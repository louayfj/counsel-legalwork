# UK Privacy Review

Use this for the active organisation's privacy, data protection, and call-recording legal
work.

## Required grounding

1. Search the active organisation's authorised reference folder for its DPA, privacy
   policy, terms, subprocessor list, call-recording wording, DPIA/LIA notes, and
   approved client responses.
2. Use UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, PECR, ICO guidance/decisions, and
   relevant FCA/customer-outcome sources where applicable.
3. Cite every legal/regulatory point and call `compliance_citation_log` before the
   final answer.

## Workflow

1. Identify roles: controller, processor, joint controller, subprocessor, or
   uncertain.
2. Identify data categories: call audio, transcripts, sales notes, CRM data,
   finance-related data, special category data, staff data, or customer data.
3. Check lawful basis, transparency, retention, access controls, subprocessors,
   international transfers, DSAR handling, deletion, breach reporting, and AI
   processing disclosures.
4. Flag sensitive or high-risk processing clearly.
5. Recommend privacy/legal review for breach, DSAR, regulator, transfer,
   biometric, special-category, or large-scale monitoring issues.

## Output

Return:

- Privacy issue summary.
- Roles and data-flow assessment.
- Risk table with source citations.
- Suggested wording or response draft where appropriate.
- Missing evidence/questions.
- Human review and DPO/escalation recommendation.
