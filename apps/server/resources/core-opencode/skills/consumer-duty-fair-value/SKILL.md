# Consumer Duty Fair-Value Statement

Use this to create or review fair-value material grounded in FCA PRIN 2A and
Consumer Duty guidance.

## Required grounding

1. Search Axleo's reference folder for `PRIN 2A fair value`, `Consumer Duty
   fair value`, and the product/service terms.
2. Cite PRIN 2A rules, FCA guidance, PS/FG documents, or the user's product evidence.
3. Call `axleo_citation_log` before the final answer.

## Workflow

1. Define the product/service, target market, distribution chain, customer group,
   pricing, commission/remuneration, non-financial costs, and expected benefits.
2. Assess the four Consumer Duty outcome angles where relevant:
   products and services, price and value, consumer understanding, consumer support.
3. Identify vulnerable-customer impacts and foreseeable harm.
4. Check evidence quality: MI, complaints, cancellations, conversion, add-on uptake,
   claims ratios, quality issues, customer feedback, and dealer controls.
5. State assumptions separately from evidence.

## Output

Produce:

- Executive summary.
- Fair-value conclusion: `reasonable basis`, `conditional`, or `not evidenced`.
- Evidence table with citations.
- Gaps and recommended MI.
- Board/senior-manager sign-off notes.
- Escalation flag for high-risk pricing, commission, redress, or vulnerable-customer harm.
